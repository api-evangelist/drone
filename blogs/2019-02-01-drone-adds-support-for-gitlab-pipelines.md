Today we’re expanding Drone’s support for portability across different CI/CD environments by announcing compatibility with the GitLab CI configuration format. This means you can use Drone to directly execute your .gitlab-ci.yml configuration file. Drone is a container-native CI/CD platform that empowers developers, and expands their choice of tools by providing portability across multiple operating systems and architectures.
