Last year we launched Drone Cloud, a free continuous integration service for the open source community. Drone Cloud runs on blazing fast x86, Arm32 and Arm64 bare metal servers donated by Packet to provide multi-architecture testing to the open source community. Since our launch we’ve helped thousands of open source projects improve support for Arm architectures, a number that continues to grow.
