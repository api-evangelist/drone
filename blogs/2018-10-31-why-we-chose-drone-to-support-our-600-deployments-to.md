QuintoAndar is an app for landlords and renters tired of the bureaucracy of the traditional apartment rental process. The Brazilian startup screens apartment hunters, acts as a guarantor for those with solid credit histories and eliminates middlemen. Founded in 2012 by two Stanford MBA Graduates, QuintoAndar is now present in 15 cities in Brazil, including São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro.
